⚠ Upload Requirements
  • Save your Excel file as CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited). This is required to properly include letters like Ñ, ñ, é, ü.
  • Make sure the CSV headers are exactly correct before uploading. Wrong or missing headers will cause upload validation errors.
  • Supported formats: student_id, last_name, first_name, middle_name, subject, section, year (middle_name optional)
    Legacy also accepted: student_id, name, subject where name = "DEL SOCORRO, Charles T" will auto-parse to DEL SOCORRO, Charles T.
